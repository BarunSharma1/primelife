About Prime Life

Big-city medicine,
neighbourly care.

Prime Life Super Multi Speciality Hospital Private Limited began in 2009 with a simple belief — no family in Fatehpur should have to travel to Kanpur or Lucknow for honest, advanced medical care.

Today, our campus on GT Road brings together senior consultants across eight departments, modern operation theatres, a fully automated pathology lab, a 24×7 emergency and a well-stocked pharmacy — all built around one promise: technology in service of human care.

  • Senior consultants, 8 specialities
  • Transparent estimates, no surprises
  • In-house lab, imaging & pharmacy
  • Cashless treatment with major TPAs

Home Services & Facilities

Everything a hospital should be — and does, daily

Six core facilities, one campus on GT Road. Expand any service below to see exactly what's inside.

Emergencies don't keep office hours, so neither does our casualty. A dedicated duty doctor, nursing team and resuscitation bays operate round the clock — with an ALS ambulance and blood storage always ready.

  • Rapid triage within minutes of arrival
  • Dedicated resuscitation bays
  • Polytrauma & accident care
  • In-house emergency pharmacy & blood storage

Our ICU combines continuous multi-parameter monitoring with round-the-clock nursing and intensivist coverage — because critical care is a rhythm, not a room.

  • Multi-parameter bed-side monitors
  • Invasive & non-invasive ventilators
  • Dedicated critical-care nursing
  • Isolation beds for infectious cases

The counter beside the emergency never closes. Every medicine is batch-verified, stored to spec, and dispensed with a pharmacist's counselling — not just a bill.

  • Round-the-counter, 365 days
  • Batch & expiry verified stock
  • Cold-chain storage for vaccines & insulin
  • Prescription counselling by pharmacists

A fully automated lab run on daily quality-control checks — so the number on your report is one your doctor can act on with confidence. Home sample collection is available across Fatehpur city.

  • Haematology, biochemistry & microbiology
  • Same-day digital reports
  • Home sample collection in city limits
  • Daily internal quality-control runs

One call brings a fully equipped Advanced Life Support ambulance to your door — oxygen, defibrillator and a trained paramedic on board — with fast dispatch across Fatehpur district.

  • ALS: oxygen, suction & defibrillator
  • Trained paramedic on every trip
  • Fast dispatch across the district
  • Intercity transfer (Kanpur / Lucknow) on request

Digital X-ray, ultrasound with colour doppler, 2D echo, ECG and TMT — every study reported by our in-house radiologist and cardiologist, so results reach your consultant the same day.

  • Digital X-Ray
  • Ultrasound & Colour Doppler
  • 2D Echo · ECG · TMT
  • Reported by in-house specialists
